# 🏰 Dungeon Zine

**Theme:** Goblin Warrens

---

## Den of the War Bugbear

A dimly lit chamber, the walls lined with crude weapons and trophies of past victories. The stench of blood and sweat lingers in the air. At the center stands a massive bugbear, scarred and battle-hardened, barking orders to the goblin warriors under his command.

**Encounter:** War bugbear sergeant leading a training session for goblin soldiers.

**Loot:** Bone dagger +1, a wickedly sharp blade believed to have been carved from the femur of a slain foe.

---

## Gobsmack Chamber

The narrow chamber reeks of goblin filth and echoes with shrill laughter. A haphazardly piled barricade of bones and broken furniture blocks the exit. A lone goblin shaman, adorned in tattered robes, dances around a flickering torch, muttering curses with eyes aglow.

**Encounter:** Goblin shaman muttering curses

**Loot:** Bone dagger +1

---

## Kobold-Infested Burrow

Flickering torchlight reveals a chaotic maze of tunnels lined with scavenged bones and makeshift barricades. Sinister chittering echoes ahead as a band of cackling kobolds prepare an ambush.

**Encounter:** A pack of 6 vicious kobolds wielding rusty daggers and makeshift slings.

**Loot:** Bag of 500 copper coins clinks ominously as it lies discarded amidst scraps of stolen trinkets and torn cloth.

---

## Chamber of Filth and Fungus

You enter a humid chamber covered in slimy moss and glowing fungi. In the corner, a goblin shaman mutters curses, surrounded by flickering candlelight. A faint aura of magic pulses from a hidden source. As you approach, the shaman's eyes lock onto you with malicious intent. 

**Encounter:** Goblin shaman casting dark magic.

**Loot:** Bone dagger +1 - a jagged weapon pulsing with malevolent energy.
